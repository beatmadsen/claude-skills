Hand-drawn sketch of a change to sharing a note, in an invented notes app; the app, its screens, its copy and its rules are all made up to show the sketch style. Today: from the note, the user taps Share, a confirmation screen asks whether they are sure, and many cancel there; then the note is shared. After the change: the confirmation screen goes, the note is shared at once with an Undo button on the screen, and the person it was shared with gets an email. Who decides: the app sends a share request, which now carries an undo deadline, to the share service, which decides. If the person is already on the note, nothing is sent, as today. If not, an undo within the deadline withdraws the share and sends nothing, and otherwise the person gets the email; both of those are new.
